# @fedemedinilla/slop-lint

## 0.2.0

First published version. It holds everything the `slop-lint/` folder holds at this release: the rule catalog, the pure `lint()` function, the CLI, the config file and its schema, inline suppressions, SARIF output and the GitHub Action. Version 0.1.0 was never published; genres, described below, are part of this first release.

Genres. `lint()` takes `genre` (`cover-letter`, the default, `email`, `follow-up` or `whatsapp`), `variety` (`rioplatense` or `neutral`) and `previous`, and the CLI takes `--genre` (also `auto`), `--variety` and `--previous`. The config file and its overrides accept `genre` and `variety`, and the action has a `genre` input. Each genre's profile lives in `GENRE_PROFILES`; the three new ones are experimental. In `email` and `follow-up` list items are FMT-01-LIST at level 2, while bold and headings stay FMT-01 at level 1. A config setting for FMT-01 applies to both, and one for FMT-01-LIST wins for the list part. A config that turns FMT-01 off lists it once among the rules off, as one rule. With a variety, REG-01 names the register the variety uses. Without a genre the output is the same as before genres existed, except for the two widenings below. New exports: `GENRES`, `GENRE_PROFILES`, `resolveRules` and `guessGenre`.

Rules only some genres run. They sit outside the catalog of 52, in `GENRE_RULES`, so a cover letter never runs them, and a config can name them under `ruleSettings`. MAIL-01 (email, follow-up, English) reports "thank you for your time and consideration" when only a sign-off and a signature, or lines that are not prose, follow it, including when it is the sign-off line itself, at level 1. MAIL-02 (email, follow-up, Spanish) reports "Estimado/a" and its other spellings with a slash, at level 2. MAIL-03 (email, follow-up, Spanish) reports "Oportunidad emocionante", the generic subject the report names, on a first line that starts with `Asunto:` or `Subject:`, at level 2. SEG-03 (follow-up, both languages) reports a run of more than 8 words of prose copied from the earlier message given in `previous` or `--previous`, leaving out the greeting and the sign-off the two share, at level 2. WA-03 (whatsapp, both languages) reports emoji, keycap numbers and flags that open two or more lines in a row as bullets, when one emoji opens two of those lines or all are keycap numbers, at level 2.

Two widenings, in every genre. REG-02 (Spanish) reports "me encantaría tener la oportunidad de", one of the calques section 11, item 4 of the report lists, as the report writes it. LEX-04 (English) counts "this matters" and "why X matters" (X of one to three words) as Tier 2 words. Section 4.1 of the report cites them from Graphite, a commercial analytics firm, tagged REPORTADO, so they are reported at severity low while the rest of LEX-04 stays medium. They come in through a new optional field of `PatternRule`, `weaker`: alternatives with their own severity and source that keep the rule's id, level, budget and place in the Tier 2 pool. A genre or config setting that changes the rule's severity changes theirs too.

Compatibility. Both widenings also run on cover letters, the default genre, so a cover letter's output changes when it holds one of the phrases. With "me encantaría tener la oportunidad de", REG-02 adds a level 2 finding at medium, which costs 1.5 score points. With "this matters" or "why X matters", `stats.tier2Used` grows by one per phrase, unless X holds a hit of LEX-04 or of another rule: then the phrase is dropped and that hit is counted and reported as before, at its own severity. When the phrase comes after the two free Tier 2 slots, or repeats, it is reported at low and costs 0.5 points. When it comes before them, it takes a free slot, and the Tier 2 word that used to fit is reported instead, at its own severity. Neither widening changes the exit code, because both are level 2. The six letters in `fixtures/cover-letters/`, the five goldens in `test/golden/` that they and the SARIF fixture feed, and `test/cover-letters.test.ts` do not change: none of those texts holds the phrases. In the labelled set, one follow-up (`follow-up-es-08`) gains a REG-02 finding. `research/genre-eval-report.md` counts it as a false positive, because the hand label of that sentence names only EST-04.
