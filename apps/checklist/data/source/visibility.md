# Visibility checks (VIS)

Passive. Technical search, link previews, basic accessibility and contact paths, as a visitor sees them. Rewritten in English from the audit method's external list.

| ID | Title | Question | Where | Pass when | If it fails | Do not touch | Priority | Area | Platforms | Needs | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VIS-01 | Indexability | Can Google show the site? | meta robots, the X-Robots-Tag header and robots.txt | The home and key pages are indexable; robots.txt blocks neither the site nor its assets | Remove the noindex in the SEO or reading settings | A noindex on staging or on private pages is correct | Critical | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-02 | robots.txt and sitemap | Does the site give Google a map of its pages? | /robots.txt and the sitemap | robots.txt declares a sitemap that answers 200 and lists real URLs | Turn the sitemap on and declare it in robots.txt | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-03 | Canonical URL | Does each page have one official address? | link rel=canonical | A self-referencing canonical on the home and an inner page, on the host of EXT-17 | Fix it in the SEO settings; use one host | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-04 | Title and meta description | Do the title and description say what the site is? | The title and meta description | The title leads with the topic in 50 to 60 characters; the description has 120 to 160 | Write them for each key page | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-05 | Heading hierarchy | Does the page have one main heading? | h1 to h3 | One h1 per page and no skipped levels | Fix it in the page builder (sliders often add extra h1 elements) | - | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-06 | Open Graph and Twitter card | Does a shared link show an image and a title? | og:title, og:description, og:image, og:locale and twitter:card | All five present; an og:image of 1200 by 630 that answers 200 | Set a default share image in the SEO settings | - | High | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-07 | Structured data | Is the organisation marked up for search engines without errors? | JSON-LD in the HTML | No errors and one Organization entity | Fix or remove inaccurate markup | Do not add markup for content the page does not show | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-08 | hreflang reciprocity | If the site has two languages, does Google know which version to show? | link rel=alternate hreflang | Each version lists every version including itself, with x-default; the URLs answer 200 and are indexable | Fix it in the SEO plugin or the template | - | Low | visibility | all | bilingual | passive |
| VIS-09 | Internal 404s and redirect chains | Are there broken links, or links that take detours? | Internal links of the home and key pages | Zero internal 404s on key pages; redirects take one hop | Fix the link or the redirect | Changing permalinks without a redirect plan | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-10 | Contrast and focus visibility | Is the text readable, and is the keyboard focus visible? | Lighthouse accessibility and a keyboard pass through the home | No contrast failures; focus visible on every link and button | Adjust colours and :focus-visible in a child theme | Editing theme CSS without a child theme | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-11 | Image alt text | Do images have a description for people who cannot see them? | alt attributes | Zero content images without alt on the home | Fill them in from the media library | - | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-12 | Reflow and zoom | Can the site be used on a narrow phone and at 200% zoom? | A 320 px wide window and 200% zoom | No horizontal scroll and no lost content | Fix fixed widths and sliders | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-13 | Form labels and target size | Are forms understandable, and are buttons easy to tap? | Public forms | Every field has a label; targets are 24 px or larger | Fix the form | Forms wired to a CRM: read the pre-change list first | Medium | visibility | all | forms | passive |
| VIS-14 | Language and title | Does the page declare its language and have its own title? | html lang and the title | lang matches the content; the title is descriptive | Set the language in the general settings and the theme | - | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-15 | Direct contact | Can a visitor call or message in one tap from a phone? | The home: phone, mail and chat links | A tel: or chat link on the home and in the footer | Add a tap-to-call or chat link to the header and footer | - | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-16 | Neglect signals | Does the site look abandoned? | The footer year, the period the home names and the date of the latest news in the feed | The footer year and the latest news are current | Update the footer, and publish news or hide the section | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-17 | Main enquiry path | Can a visitor reach the main enquiry page from the home? | Links on the home (admissions, bookings or quotes) | The home links to the main enquiry page | Add it to the menu and the home | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-18 | Messaging link number format | Do the chat buttons open the chat? | Messaging links on the home | Numbers in international format with no extra characters | Rewrite the number in international format | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-19 | Template leftovers | Are defaults from the theme or the CMS still published? | Sample pages, the default tagline and demo content | No default content is published | Remove or replace it | - | Low | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-20 | Legacy browser code | Is the code written for browsers nobody uses any more? | The home HTML | No Internet Explorer conditional comments, old polyfills or Flash | Remove them from the theme | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
| VIS-21 | Published mail domains receive mail | Can the published mail addresses receive mail? | mailto: links on the home and the contact page | Every published domain has MX, or A or AAAA as the fallback, and no null MX | Fix the address or the domain's mail records | - | Medium | visibility | all | - | passive |
